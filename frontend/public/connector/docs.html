Unify connector for Claude

Manage your Unify business by chatting with Claude: see the day ahead, find free slots, book and reschedule, check guests in, read your dashboards and approve AI-planned schedules.

Connector URL: https://se-3090-se-002.vercel.app/mcp
Sign-in: your Unify account (OAuth). Works in: Claude on the web, desktop and mobile, and Claude Code.

What you need

  • A Unify business account with the Admin, Manager or Staff role. Sign up at se-3090-se-002.vercel.app/register.
  • A Claude plan that includes connectors.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors. Find Unify in the directory, or choose Add custom connector and paste https://se-3090-se-002.vercel.app/mcp.
  2. Click Connect. A Unify sign-in page opens.
  3. Sign in with your Unify email and password. Leave Allow changes ticked to let Claude create and change bookings, or untick it for view-only access.
  4. In a new chat, try "Who am I in Unify?"

Your password goes only to Unify. Claude receives a token that lets it act as you, within your role and your business only.

Things to ask

  • "What does tomorrow look like at the clinic?"
  • "Find Dr. Perera's free slots on Friday afternoon and book Nimal Silva for a 30-minute check-up."
  • "Move booking 7f3e… to next Tuesday at 10:00."
  • "Are there any double-bookings this week?"
  • "Show this month's revenue and no-show rate."
  • "Which inventory items are low on stock?"
  • "Show pending AI planner workflows and reject the one that books outside opening hours."

Permissions and safety

  • Claude acts as the Unify user who signed in, so Unify's own role rules apply. A Staff account can't do what only an Admin can.
  • Data stays inside your business (tenant). The owner-only platform console and account/password endpoints aren't available through the connector.
  • View-only access: untick Allow changes when signing in, or connect with https://se-3090-se-002.vercel.app/mcp?readonly=1.
  • Times are read and shown in business-local time (Asia/Colombo).

Disconnect

In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Unify → Disconnect. Claude then deletes its tokens.

Troubleshooting

  • The first request is slow, or the sign-in takes a while. Unify's server sleeps when idle and can take up to a minute to wake up. Try again after a minute.
  • The sign-in page shows "404 – Page Not Found". An old cached copy of the Unify web app answered. Refresh the page once.
  • "Your Unify role is not allowed to do this". The action needs a higher Unify role, such as Admin or Manager.
  • "Your Unify session ended". Disconnect and connect again.
